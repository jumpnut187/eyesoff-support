# Eyes Off: Support

Eyes Off is a macOS menu bar app that keeps what's on your screen private: an owner-locked blur (Touch ID to remove), automatic blur when someone looks over your shoulder, and a focus mode that blurs everything except your active window.

## Get help

[Open an issue](https://github.com/jumpnut187/eyesoff-support/issues/new) describing what happened, your macOS version, and your Mac model. Bug reports and feature requests are both welcome.

## Common questions

**Where is the app?** Eyes Off has no Dock icon. Look for the eye icon in the menu bar.

**I can't remove the blur.** On a Mac with Touch ID, put your finger on the sensor. If Touch ID is unavailable (locked out, or the lid is closed), press the shortcut again (default Control-Option-Command-B).

**Onlooker detection isn't working.** Make sure camera access is allowed in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera, and that "Blur When Someone Looks" is checked in the menu.

## Privacy policy

Eyes Off collects no data.

- It never reads, records or transmits your screen. It only draws its own blur on top.
- If you turn on onlooker detection, camera frames are analysed on your Mac with Apple's Vision framework to count faces, then discarded immediately. Frames are never stored or sent anywhere.
- Eyes Off has no network access, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
- Settings (shortcuts, dimming, toggles) are stored locally on your Mac.
